Support

Need help with One Pocket?

Use this page for app support, bug reports, purchase questions, and general feedback.

Contact

Email capydev.sys@outlook.com — One Pocket is made by one person, and that inbox reaches them directly. We aim to respond as soon as possible.

Helpful Details

When reporting a problem, include your device model, operating system version (iOS or Android), One Pocket app version (shown in Settings → About), and a short description of what happened.

Purchases

Billing and refunds are managed directly by the store you bought through — Apple on the App Store or Google on Google Play. Contact us if One Pocket Pro did not unlock after a purchase or restore, and we'll help sort it out.

Your Data

One Pocket stores everything on your device — there is no account to recover and no server copy of your budgets. See the privacy policy for details.
