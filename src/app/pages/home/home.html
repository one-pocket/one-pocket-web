One number, not a dashboard

What's left is the only headline. No charts, no categories fighting for attention — open the app, read one number, close it.

Log an expense in seconds

A purpose-built keypad with arithmetic where you need it: type 3 × 120 for three nights and let the app do the math.

Spend in any currency

Declare the currencies a budget uses and the rate you got. Entries convert to your home currency, so the one number stays honest abroad.

Finished budgets become memories

Complete a budget and it moves to the archive — a small record of the trip you took or the thing you saved for, not a spreadsheet to maintain forever.

Private by design

No account, no sign-up, no tracking. Your budgets live on your device. Read the privacy policy — it's short because there's little to explain.

Free to use. Pro if you need more.

One Pocket is free with two active budgets. Pro unlocks unlimited budgets for a one-time purchase — no subscription, because a budgeting app that charges you monthly is a bill.
