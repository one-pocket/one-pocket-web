Privacy Policy

One Pocket keeps your budgets on your device.

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Overview

One Pocket helps you set a budget for one purpose, log expenses against it, and see what's left. This policy explains what information the app stores and how it is used. The short version: your data stays on your device, and we don't collect it.

Information You Add

You can enter budget names, amounts, currencies and exchange rates, expenses, funding adjustments, categories, dates, and notes into the app.

This information is used only to provide app functionality: calculating what's left, organizing your ledger, and keeping completed budgets in your archive.

Local Storage

One Pocket is a local-first app. Your budgets and settings are stored on your device. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no server that receives your data.

No Tracking or Analytics

One Pocket does not include analytics, advertising identifiers, or third-party tracking of any kind. The app works fully offline.

Purchases

One Pocket offers a one-time Pro upgrade as an in-app purchase, processed by the store you bought through — Apple through the App Store on iOS, or Google through Google Play on Android. Your purchase status is used to unlock features and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment details.

Your Data and Deletion

You control your data. You can delete any budget, expense, or archived record inside the app, which removes it from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all app data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
